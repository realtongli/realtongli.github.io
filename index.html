About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in the School of Science, Engineering and Digital Technologies at the University of Southern Queensland (UniSQ), Australia.

My research develops deep learning methods, particularly capsule networks, for reliable detection and diagnosis in critical systems. My current work focuses on zero-day API attack detection with runtime-updatable models. Previously, I worked on fault diagnosis of power transformers using dissolved gas analysis (DGA), domain knowledge integration, and generative data augmentation.

Before joining UniSQ, I received my Bachelor's and Master's degrees from North China Electric Power University (NCEPU).

I am open to research collaborations and to academic and industry opportunities in AI for cybersecurity and intelligent energy systems.

Research Interests

  • AI for Cybersecurity: zero-day API attack detection, adaptive and updatable detection models
  • Capsule Networks and Deep Representation Learning
  • Fault Diagnosis of Power Equipment: dissolved gas analysis, knowledge-guided learning
  • Data Augmentation with Generative Models for Imbalanced and Small Datasets
